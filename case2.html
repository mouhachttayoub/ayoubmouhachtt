The brief

The business context

An app competes for attention before it earns a place in someone’s routine. For Beloud, acquisition covers Meta, Google app campaigns and Apple Search Ads, with creative concepts that make the app’s appeal understandable before the install.

The growth question

How do you turn a compelling app into a repeatable acquisition story? The first challenge is earning the right attention. The next is understanding whether the people who arrive find value, rather than reading every install as the same outcome.

My focus

Connect creative concepts with channel intent and the journey into the app. The work covers app acquisition and creative testing; the reported growth milestone gives that scope a concrete commercial context.

The work behind the headline

Each part of the journey needs a clear purpose. Here is how the priorities connect to the business question.

PriorityExecution focusWhat to look for
Creative conceptsConnect a user motivation to an angle that makes the app worth exploring.Does the concept attract interest in the app itself?
Channel executionBring the app story to Meta, Google app campaigns and Apple Search Ads.How does intent change across discovery and search?
Testing & iterationMake creative comparisons useful by being clear about the idea each concept tests.What is the evidence telling us about the message?
Beyond the installRead acquisition alongside how people use the app and whether they return.Does volume translate into a valuable user relationship?

The lessons I bring to the next project

Creative is part of the strategy.

The message decides what kind of attention a campaign earns, not just how an ad looks.

Channels carry different intent.

App discovery and app search are different moments. The story should reflect why someone is there.

An install starts the relationship.

The next question is what the user does with the app. That context makes acquisition decisions more useful.
