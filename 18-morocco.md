@@ cod-stockout-ad-spend
META: Prevent COD stockouts from wasting advertising by reconciling sellable stock, reservations and campaign exposure before new orders are accepted.
ANSWER: COD stockout control connects advertising to stock that is genuinely available after reservations. Pausing a product page alone may not stop ads or queued confirmations. Update every touchpoint and handle existing orders transparently under the actual service process.
RELATED: ecommerce-inventory-marketing,cod-order-confirmation-workflow,ecommerce-paid-traffic-capacity

## Separate warehouse stock from free stock

Count usable units, then subtract committed orders and any unavailable items. A COD order can reserve inventory before money is collected. Make the reservation rule and release conditions explicit in the order system.

Check variants separately. Having units of one color does not make another color available. Avoid substituting an item after confirmation without explaining the actual options to the customer.

## Worked example: forty units are not forty new sales

Illustrative example: a warehouse has forty usable units, and thirty are already reserved. Only ten remain free under the stated reservation rule. A campaign forecast of twenty additional orders exceeds that availability by ten.

| Inventory state | Illustrative units |
| Usable stock | 40 |
| Existing reservations | 30 |
| Free stock | 10 |
| Proposed new demand | 20 |
| Potential shortage | 10 |

Replenishment may change the decision only when its timing and terms support the customer promise. An uncertain supplier estimate is not confirmed stock.

## Update the whole journey

Coordinate advertisements, feed availability, product pages, checkout, confirmation scripts and support. Assign someone to verify that old assets no longer advertise the unavailable variant. Cached or scheduled promotion can continue after a page update.

For existing orders, record the actual resolution and customer agreement. Do not mark an unfulfilled order delivered or quietly change its variant merely to improve the dashboard.

## Review the commercial effect

Include wasted advertising, support effort, canceled orders and later delivery outcomes. A stockout-related conversion decline should not automatically trigger new creative or higher bids.

Build an availability check into the campaign readiness process. Use the [inventory guide](ecommerce-inventory-marketing.html) for the data view and the [capacity guide](ecommerce-paid-traffic-capacity.html) when stock is available but handling is constrained.

@@ multilingual-cod-call-script
META: Structure a multilingual COD confirmation call around the same product, amount and delivery promise, with English, French and Arabic clarification examples.
ANSWER: A multilingual COD confirmation script should preserve one accurate purchase promise across languages. Confirm the product, variant, amount, destination and supported next step. Adapt the wording to the customer without changing what the business will deliver.
RELATED: bilingual-copy-review,cod-product-disclosure,cod-address-quality

## Use the order record as the factual reference

Before the call, check the current item, quantity, charges, address and stock status. The agent should not improvise delivery timing or a discount to obtain confirmation. If a detail is unresolved, record the clarification before dispatch.

Choose the language the customer understands. English, French, standard Arabic and Moroccan Darija are not interchangeable registers. A natural conversation can adapt its tone while retaining the same conditions.

## Organize the call by customer task

| Step | Purpose |
| Introduction | Explain who is calling and which order is involved |
| Purchase recap | Verify item, variant, quantity and amount |
| Destination check | Resolve the route-relevant details |
| Delivery explanation | State supported timing and conditions |
| Decision | Record confirmation, correction or cancellation accurately |

Keep questions proportionate. Do not collect unrelated personal details or turn a correction into pressure to accept the order.

## Example clarification in three languages

Illustrative wording, to use with the actual order details: “Could you confirm that the item, quantity and total amount match what you intended to order?”

French: “Pouvez-vous confirmer que l’article, la quantité et le montant total correspondent à votre commande ?”

Standard Arabic: “هل يمكنك تأكيد أن المنتج والكمية والمبلغ الإجمالي تطابق ما أردت طلبه؟”

These examples ask the same question. Insert the verified details in the conversation rather than expecting the customer to remember an unclear advertisement.

## Preserve the agreement after the call

Update the shared order record with corrections and the next action. Stop duplicate confirmation requests when the task is complete. Ensure picking and packing use the corrected information, not the earlier form submission.

Review sampled calls where permitted and compare the promise with delivered outcomes. Use the [bilingual review guide](bilingual-copy-review.html) when translated scripts gradually acquire different terms.

@@ regional-delivery-promise
META: Write regional delivery promises in Morocco using actual courier coverage, dispatch timing and customer conditions instead of an unsupported nationwide estimate.
ANSWER: A regional delivery promise should reflect the route the business can actually serve. Distinguish dispatch from delivery, state the supported conditions and verify exceptions before accepting the order. One successful nearby delivery does not establish a nationwide schedule.
RELATED: cod-courier-comparison,ecommerce-preorder-messaging,cod-product-disclosure

## Build a service map from real operations

Use actual courier coverage and order records. Group destinations only when they share relevant service conditions. Do not create a public list of delivery times from assumptions or copy another merchant's promises.

Record cut-off rules, handling time and known limitations internally. The public wording should explain what affects the customer without exposing operational details that do not help their decision.

## Check the promise fields

| Field | Customer-facing question |
| Coverage | Is this destination actually supported? |
| Dispatch | When is the parcel expected to leave? |
| Arrival | What estimate is supported for this route? |
| Charges | What amount will the customer pay? |
| Exceptions | Which conditions change the estimate? |
| Updates | How will the customer learn about a change? |

Treat an estimate as an estimate. Do not call a variable route guaranteed unless the business genuinely offers that commitment.

## Worked example: nearby delivery becomes a blanket headline

Illustrative example: a store completes several nearby deliveries quickly and advertises the same timing to all destinations. A more distant route has a different schedule, creating refusals and support questions. The repair is route-specific information before confirmation, not blaming the customer for the delay.

Keep advertisements and confirmation agents aligned with the current service map. An accurate product page cannot compensate for a caller making a stronger unsupported promise.

## Review mature route outcomes

Compare comparable dispatch cohorts, actual arrival timing and exceptions. Separate merchant handling delays from courier movement where the records allow it. Avoid reporting unresolved recent parcels as failed deliveries.

Update the promise when operating conditions change and communicate appropriately with existing buyers. Use the [courier comparison guide](cod-courier-comparison.html) to assess the relevant service evidence.

@@ cod-repeat-customer-profit
META: Assess repeat COD customer contribution using collected orders, fulfillment and refusal costs before offering loyalty discounts or increasing retention spend.
ANSWER: Repeat COD customers can be valuable, but their profitability still depends on collected orders, variable costs and retention effort. Compare mature repeat cohorts with consistent definitions. Familiar customers do not automatically make every discount or delivery attempt profitable.
RELATED: first-order-vs-repeat-customer-profit,cod-cash-cycle,loyalty-program-value-vs-discounts

## Define the repeat customer and outcome

Use a customer identity the business can support, and explain unresolved matching. An order from the same phone may need careful reconciliation before it is labeled a new or returning relationship.

Count delivered and collected outcomes under the actual process. Separate placed orders, confirmations and final payments. Pending repeat orders have not yet established contribution.

## Include retention and servicing costs

| Cost or value | What to record |
| Collected revenue | Actual mature payment basis |
| Product and delivery | Costs associated with fulfilled and unsuccessful attempts |
| Discount | Reflected once in the chosen model |
| Contact effort | Relevant retention and confirmation cost |
| Returns | Refunds, reverse logistics and inventory recovery |

Do not deduct the same discount from revenue and again as an expense.

## Worked example: the loyalty discount erases contribution

Illustrative example in MAD: a repeat order contributes 40 MAD before a new loyalty incentive. A 25 MAD discount and 10 MAD additional contact or servicing cost leave 5 MAD under the simplified model. A larger order count would not by itself establish stronger total contribution.

If the discount encourages purchases that would have happened anyway, its incremental value can be weaker still. Use a suitable comparison where feasible rather than treating every redeemed offer as additional demand.

## Compare the right customers

Match product, route and cohort age. A repeat-customer group may contain better addresses or easier destinations than a new-customer group. Those differences can explain delivery performance without proving loyalty caused it.

Create a repeat-order contribution sheet and include cash collection timing. Evaluate the offer after returns and remittance mature. The [first-order versus repeat guide](first-order-vs-repeat-customer-profit.html) helps keep acquisition and later value separate.

@@ morocco-growth-consultation-preparation
META: Prepare for a marketing consultation with your offer, customer journey, useful numbers and one clear decision, including the portfolio's actual booking path.
ANSWER: A useful marketing consultation starts with a specific business question and a small set of relevant evidence. Bring the offer, audience, current channels and the decision you need to make. A long dashboard export is less useful than clear definitions and the context behind the numbers.
RELATED: marketing-growth-audit,fully-loaded-customer-acquisition-cost,cod-order-dashboard

## Choose the decision before collecting files

Examples include whether to scale a campaign, repair a conversion obstacle or change a growth priority. Describe the current constraint and what a useful answer would allow you to do. Avoid expecting one conversation to replace a complete campaign implementation.

For a Moroccan COD store, distinguish placed, confirmed, delivered and collected orders. For SaaS, distinguish registrations, activated accounts and paying customers. These stages can lead to very different recommendations.

## Prepare a concise brief

| Item | What to bring |
| Offer | Product, scope, actual price and conditions |
| Customer | Buying situation and current alternative |
| Journey | Steps from discovery to a useful outcome |
| Numbers | Counts, costs and reporting windows with definitions |
| Constraint | The obstacle you want to investigate |
| Capacity | Budget, team and operational limits |

Remove unnecessary personal or confidential customer information. Screenshots should provide context without disclosing data you are not permitted to share.

## Worked example: “ads do not work” becomes a useful question

Illustrative example: a store reports cheap orders but weak collected revenue. The consultation question becomes whether the problem lies in expectations, confirmation, delivery or acquisition fit. Bringing mature order states and variable costs makes that diagnosis more useful than an isolated ad screenshot.

The consultation can then identify the next evidence or action instead of recommending another campaign before understanding the current one.

## Use the actual booking process

The portfolio's [consultation plan](../plans.html#consultation) lists one session at $50 USD. Read its current payment and booking instructions. Use consistent contact details for payment and scheduling, and confirm the actual meeting arrangements rather than assuming a duration not stated in the offer.

Bring the brief and one priority question. If the outcome requires ongoing implementation, review the separate support plans rather than expecting that work to be included automatically.
