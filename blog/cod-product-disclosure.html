Morocco & COD

COD product descriptions: prevent mismatched expectations at delivery

Clarify COD product descriptions with accurate inclusions, variants, dimensions and delivery conditions so buyers know what to expect at the door.

Ayoub’s Growth Notebook2 min readPrepared 13 October 2026AI-assisted guide
The short answerCOD product disclosure should align the advertisement, order page, confirmation and delivered item. Explain what is included, the chosen variant and the payable amount before dispatch. Better disclosure helps prevent expectation mismatch; it cannot eliminate every refusal.

Make the purchase concrete

Show the actual product and supported specifications. Distinguish accessories or props from included items. Keep variants, dimensions and quantity visible. A dramatic image should not imply a larger item or additional components the customer will not receive.

State the total amount and actual delivery conditions. If charges vary by supported destination, resolve the amount before the customer's final commitment. Do not leave the courier to explain an unexpected increase.

Use an expectation checklist

DetailConsistency check
ProductAdvertisement and order record describe the same item
VariantCustomer choice matches picking and packing
QuantityImage and copy do not imply more units
DimensionsUnits and scale are clear
Total payableIncludes the relevant disclosed charges
DeliveryTiming and conditions match actual service

Check the confirmation message too. Accurate page copy can be undone by an agent making a different promise.

Worked example: the image suggests a set

Illustrative example: an ad shows three items, while the price covers one. A customer orders expecting the set and refuses the parcel. Calling this only a “low-quality lead” misses the communication failure.

Clarify the purchased quantity in the main creative, page and confirmation. If several package options exist, make the chosen package visible in the order record. Use actual product photographs or accurately labeled mockups.

Learn from mature refusal reasons

Review recorded expectation-related refusals alongside the original ad and order. Compare like products and periods. A campaign can create more orders while worsening delivered contribution if its main promise is misunderstood.

Choose one recurring mismatch and repair every touchpoint carrying it. Use the return-reason guide to separate marketing, fulfillment and product causes.
