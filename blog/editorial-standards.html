About the notebook

Useful answers. Clear evidence.

How the new growth and marketing guides are prepared, and how to interpret their examples.

Purpose and authorship

Ayoub’s Growth Notebook is part of his marketing portfolio. The new collection was prepared with AI assistance to explain practical questions about growth, acquisition, product value and customer communication. These commissioned guides are educational material; they do not claim that Ayoub personally conducted every hypothetical experiment described.

The portfolio’s About and Work pages provide his professional context. The guides link to those pages separately from teaching examples.

Examples and evidence

Numerical worked examples are labeled illustrative and state their assumptions. They demonstrate calculations or decisions; they are not market benchmarks, promised outcomes or newly reported client results.

Project evidence belongs in the corresponding case study. Confidential commercial information is not used to fill gaps in an article. Missing growth-history points are not invented.

Platform information and sources

Relevant official documentation is linked where a guide discusses platform definitions, technical requirements or implementation context. Strategic checklists and decision frameworks are editorial guidance, not claims that a platform endorses the approach.

Interfaces, policies and measurement options can change. Confirm current primary-source details before implementing consequential platform changes.

Search visibility

The articles use descriptive titles, readable answers, real internal links and metadata that reflects the visible page. These foundations support discovery and understanding. They do not guarantee indexing, rankings, traffic or inclusion in an AI-generated answer.

Languages and maintenance

The 100 new guides are in English. Existing French and Arabic articles remain available in their own collections. A language link to a collection is labeled as navigation and is not represented as a translation of an English-only article.

Dates identify the new material’s preparation. Existing articles have not been given artificial new publication dates. Future substantive changes should update both the answer and its modification record.
