Morocco & COD

COD stockouts and advertising: stop buying demand for unavailable products

Prevent COD stockouts from wasting advertising by reconciling sellable stock, reservations and campaign exposure before new orders are accepted.

Ayoub’s Growth Notebook2 min readPrepared 13 October 2026AI-assisted guide
The short answerCOD stockout control connects advertising to stock that is genuinely available after reservations. Pausing a product page alone may not stop ads or queued confirmations. Update every touchpoint and handle existing orders transparently under the actual service process.

Separate warehouse stock from free stock

Count usable units, then subtract committed orders and any unavailable items. A COD order can reserve inventory before money is collected. Make the reservation rule and release conditions explicit in the order system.

Check variants separately. Having units of one color does not make another color available. Avoid substituting an item after confirmation without explaining the actual options to the customer.

Worked example: forty units are not forty new sales

Illustrative example: a warehouse has forty usable units, and thirty are already reserved. Only ten remain free under the stated reservation rule. A campaign forecast of twenty additional orders exceeds that availability by ten.

Inventory stateIllustrative units
Usable stock40
Existing reservations30
Free stock10
Proposed new demand20
Potential shortage10

Replenishment may change the decision only when its timing and terms support the customer promise. An uncertain supplier estimate is not confirmed stock.

Update the whole journey

Coordinate advertisements, feed availability, product pages, checkout, confirmation scripts and support. Assign someone to verify that old assets no longer advertise the unavailable variant. Cached or scheduled promotion can continue after a page update.

For existing orders, record the actual resolution and customer agreement. Do not mark an unfulfilled order delivered or quietly change its variant merely to improve the dashboard.

Review the commercial effect

Include wasted advertising, support effort, canceled orders and later delivery outcomes. A stockout-related conversion decline should not automatically trigger new creative or higher bids.

Build an availability check into the campaign readiness process. Use the inventory guide for the data view and the capacity guide when stock is available but handling is constrained.
