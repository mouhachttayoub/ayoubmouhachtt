The brief

The business context

For a SaaS business, a good campaign has to lead somewhere useful. eGrow’s public product offering includes automation workflows and WhatsApp tools for commerce. That breadth makes a clear audience, a clear use case and a clear next step especially important.

The growth question

How do you help the right prospect understand the product without overwhelming them? The growth problem sits across positioning, high-intent acquisition, outbound, conversion and the first product experience. Treating each part separately leaves gaps in the journey.

My focus

Connect the promise in the campaign with the page that explains it and the onboarding that helps a new customer use it. The work spans audience refinement, Google Ads, outbound, conversion optimization and activation.

The work behind the headline

Each part of the journey needs a clear purpose. Here is how the priorities connect to the business question.

Audience & positioningRefine the customer problem, use case and promise so the product is easier to understand.Do the message and use case match the customer’s intent?

The lessons I bring to the next project

Specific beats broad.

A prospect needs to recognize their own problem before they need to understand every product feature.

The handoff matters.

The ad, page and first product experience should make the same promise in language the customer understands.

Volume needs context.

Traffic and sign-ups become useful signals when you understand who they bring in and what happens next.
