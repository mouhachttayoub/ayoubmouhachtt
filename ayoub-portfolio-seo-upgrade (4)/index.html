What I actually solve

Before we talk

A few things you might ask.

Who do you work with?

Mostly SaaS, mobile apps and e-commerce businesses. If you have a real product and a growth challenge, let’s talk.

Do you handle strategy and execution?

Yes. I can help define the plan and get the work moving—from positioning and campaigns to creative, conversion and reporting.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Marrakech and work remotely. We’ll agree on a rhythm that suits your team.

How do we get started?

Send me your website, what you want to improve and where you’re stuck. We’ll work out the right scope together.

How much does it cost?

That depends on the work and level of involvement. Explore the plans, then get in touch for a proposal.

Are you open to a Head of Marketing role?

Yes. Share the company, the role and what you’re hoping to build by email or LinkedIn.

What access will you need?

We agree on access based on the scope, usually the relevant ad accounts, analytics and funnel information. Your business keeps ownership of its accounts.

How will we review the work?

We agree on a review rhythm and clear metric definitions. The aim is to explain what happened, what we learned and what we should do next.

Why are some case-study figures private?

Client confidentiality matters. I can explain the work and responsibilities without publishing private financial data.

Can you help with a focused project?

Yes. A campaign audit, creative testing, a landing-page review or a measurement problem can be a useful starting point.

What is stopping your growth right now?

Send me the product, the channels you are using and the number you want to move. I would rather understand the bottleneck first than sell you a generic package.
