Before we work together

How do I pay and book the consultation?

Pay $50 USD through the PayPal link above, then return here and choose a time using the booking link. Use the same email for both. I’ll check the payment before confirming your appointment. The consultation provides advice; campaign management and implementation are separate services.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose the consultation for focused advice before deciding what to do next. If ads need an owner, choose Media Buying. If the challenge spans the funnel, explore Business Growth. A defined project fits a Custom Partnership.

Is ad spend included?

Media budget and service fees are separate. We agree on spend, tools and other costs in the proposal before work begins.

How long will we work together?

We agree on the duration and milestones after reviewing your needs. A focused audit has a different scope from ongoing growth support.

Who owns the accounts and work?

Your business retains its accounts. We define access, deliverables and handover in the agreed scope.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We agree who owns creative, development, approvals and reporting so the plan fits the people doing the work.

What should I send before a call?

Your website, main offer, target audience, current channels and the question you most want to resolve. A rough budget range helps shape the scope.

What is stopping your growth right now?

Send me the product, the channels you are using and the number you want to move. I would rather understand the bottleneck first than sell you a generic package.
