@@ cod-cash-cycle
META: Plan a Moroccan COD store's cash cycle around inventory, advertising, delivery and courier remittance, using a clearly illustrative MAD example.
ANSWER: A COD cash cycle follows money from upfront acquisition and stock costs to delivery and usable remittance. An order placed is not collected revenue. Use your actual courier terms and mature order states to decide how much new advertising the business can fund.
RELATED: ad-spend-cash-flow,cod-order-dashboard,cod-repeat-customer-profit

## Map the dates that affect cash

Record when stock, advertising, packaging and courier charges are paid, then when delivered-order funds become usable. Terms differ by the actual service agreement. Do not assume a single remittance schedule applies to every Moroccan courier.

Separate delivered, refused, returned and pending orders. Keep inventory recovery distinct from cash collection. A returned item can remain an asset while still leaving the business short of spendable cash.

## Worked example: stock cash is not the same as product expense

Illustrative example in MAD: a store purchases fifty units at 100 MAD each and spends the amounts below before receiving COD collections. Forty units are delivered at 200 MAD each; ten are returned and remain sellable under the example's assumptions.

| Upfront payment | Illustrative amount |
| Stock purchase | 5,000 MAD |
| Advertising | 2,000 MAD |
| Packaging | 500 MAD |
| Courier charges under assumed terms | 1,000 MAD |
| Total paid before collection | 8,500 MAD |

The forty deliveries collect 8,000 MAD. That cash view is not a final loss calculation: ten units remain in inventory. A contribution model would use the appropriate sold-product cost and other expenses consistently. The example illustrates funding timing, not courier prices or typical market performance.

## Forecast with unresolved outcomes visible

Show pending collections separately from confirmed available cash. Test what happens if remittance arrives later or more parcels return. Avoid funding the next campaign from gross order value that may never be collected.

Review the forecast against actual courier statements and bank records. Differences can arise from fees, timing, adjustments or unresolved parcels. Trace known order IDs before changing campaign budgets based on an unexplained total.

## Set a spending boundary

Estimate the cash needed to fulfill the additional demand before collections arrive. Maintain an operating reserve appropriate to the business. Assign someone to pause new exposure when the available cash or fulfillment constraint is reached.

Build a weekly collection timeline using actual terms, not this example's figures. Pair it with the [COD order dashboard](cod-order-dashboard.html) so pending outcomes do not disappear inside a revenue total.

@@ cod-courier-comparison
META: Compare COD couriers with matched, mature shipment cohorts, consistent status definitions and total contribution instead of one delivery percentage.
ANSWER: Compare COD couriers using similar product, region and customer cohorts at the same maturity. Review delivery outcomes, timing, fees, returns and remittance together. A higher reported delivery rate is not a fair comparison if one courier serves easier routes or counts statuses differently.
RELATED: cod-delivery-rate,cod-cash-cycle,regional-delivery-promise

## Align the shipment populations

Record dispatch date, region, product, order value and relevant confirmation state. Define delivered, refused, returned and unresolved using reconciled records. “Attempted” and “delivered” must not become interchangeable labels.

Use actual service coverage and terms. Do not rank Moroccan couriers from an unverified anecdote or assume one operator's pricing applies to another. The right comparison is the outcome for your business's supported routes.

## Build the comparison sheet

| Dimension | What to compare |
| Cohort | Similar dispatch periods and customer conditions |
| Mature delivery | Resolved outcomes under one definition |
| Timing | Dispatch-to-delivery and collection timing |
| Cost | Applicable delivery, refusal and return charges |
| Service | Tracking accuracy and issue resolution |
| Contribution | Collected revenue after relevant variable costs |

Keep unresolved parcels separate. Recent shipments have not had the same opportunity to finish as older ones.

## Worked example: two percentages hide different routes

Illustrative example: Courier A delivers eighty of one hundred mature shipments and B delivers eighty-two. If B receives only nearby, well-confirmed orders while A receives harder routes, the two-point difference does not establish better courier performance.

Compare matched route and product groups, or use a suitable planned allocation where operationally feasible. Small differences can also reflect ordinary variation. Review evidence strength before moving all volume.

## Include the cash and customer experience

A delivery can be commercially weaker if collection is slow, fees are higher or customers experience repeated confusion. Reconcile invoices and remittance rather than relying only on a tracking dashboard.

Start with one comparable shipment group and trace known parcels through final outcomes and charges. Use the [delivery-rate guide](cod-delivery-rate.html) for denominator consistency and the [cash-cycle guide](cod-cash-cycle.html) for funding implications.

@@ cod-address-quality
META: Improve COD address quality through useful confirmation, destination clarity and consistent order records without blaming customers or collecting unnecessary details.
ANSWER: COD address quality means the delivery information is sufficient and consistent for the actual service route. Check missing or ambiguous details before dispatch. A confirmation conversation should clarify the parcel's destination and expectations, not merely repeat “Is your order confirmed?”
RELATED: cod-order-confirmation-workflow,multilingual-cod-call-script,cod-follow-up-frequency

## Identify the information the route needs

Use the courier's actual supported address requirements and coverage. Keep city, locality and relevant delivery details consistent across the order form, confirmation record and parcel label. Ask only for information needed to fulfill the task.

Do not infer that every refused order was caused by a bad address. Price surprise, product mismatch, timing and changed need require different investigation. Keep the recorded reason and suspected cause separate.

## Review common ambiguities

| Address issue | Useful clarification |
| Missing destination detail | Ask for the route-relevant information |
| Conflicting city or locality | Confirm the intended destination |
| Unclear delivery contact | Confirm the appropriate contact through the supported process |
| Unsupported area | Explain the actual service limit before dispatch |
| Customer unavailable | Agree the supported next step or timing |

Do not promise an appointment slot the courier cannot provide.

## Worked example: confirmation without destination clarity

Illustrative example: a customer confirms purchase intent, but the address combines a city with a locality served through another route. The order moves to dispatch because the only required field is “confirmed.” Adding a specific destination check can reveal the issue before the parcel leaves.

Record the clarification in the order system, not only in a private chat. Otherwise the warehouse can print the original address and erase the benefit of the conversation.

## Evaluate the process rather than one call count

Review address-related delivery exceptions, repeat contact attempts and mature outcomes. A longer confirmation call may be worthwhile when it prevents repeated delivery confusion, but unnecessary questions can also create friction.

Audit a small set of recent problematic parcels and identify the exact missing information. Repair the form or handover step that lost it. Use the [multilingual script](multilingual-cod-call-script.html) to keep the clarification consistent across customer languages.

@@ cod-product-disclosure
META: Clarify COD product descriptions with accurate inclusions, variants, dimensions and delivery conditions so buyers know what to expect at the door.
ANSWER: COD product disclosure should align the advertisement, order page, confirmation and delivered item. Explain what is included, the chosen variant and the payable amount before dispatch. Better disclosure helps prevent expectation mismatch; it cannot eliminate every refusal.
RELATED: ecommerce-return-reason-insights,ecommerce-product-page-copy,cod-refusal-and-return-analysis

## Make the purchase concrete

Show the actual product and supported specifications. Distinguish accessories or props from included items. Keep variants, dimensions and quantity visible. A dramatic image should not imply a larger item or additional components the customer will not receive.

State the total amount and actual delivery conditions. If charges vary by supported destination, resolve the amount before the customer's final commitment. Do not leave the courier to explain an unexpected increase.

## Use an expectation checklist

| Detail | Consistency check |
| Product | Advertisement and order record describe the same item |
| Variant | Customer choice matches picking and packing |
| Quantity | Image and copy do not imply more units |
| Dimensions | Units and scale are clear |
| Total payable | Includes the relevant disclosed charges |
| Delivery | Timing and conditions match actual service |

Check the confirmation message too. Accurate page copy can be undone by an agent making a different promise.

## Worked example: the image suggests a set

Illustrative example: an ad shows three items, while the price covers one. A customer orders expecting the set and refuses the parcel. Calling this only a “low-quality lead” misses the communication failure.

Clarify the purchased quantity in the main creative, page and confirmation. If several package options exist, make the chosen package visible in the order record. Use actual product photographs or accurately labeled mockups.

## Learn from mature refusal reasons

Review recorded expectation-related refusals alongside the original ad and order. Compare like products and periods. A campaign can create more orders while worsening delivered contribution if its main promise is misunderstood.

Choose one recurring mismatch and repair every touchpoint carrying it. Use the [return-reason guide](ecommerce-return-reason-insights.html) to separate marketing, fulfillment and product causes.

@@ cod-follow-up-frequency
META: Coordinate COD calls and messages around the current order state, with a practical follow-up ledger that prevents duplicate or outdated contact attempts.
ANSWER: COD follow-up should respond to the current order task: clarification, confirmation, dispatch or a delivery issue. Coordinate calls and messages so different people do not repeat the same request. Contact volume is not a substitute for accurate information and a clear next step.
RELATED: cod-address-quality,lifecycle-frequency-caps,whatsapp-ecommerce-order-follow-up

## Keep one current order record

Record contact attempts, customer replies and the agreed next action in the system used by the team. A private conversation should not leave another agent believing the order is still unconfirmed.

Distinguish purchase confirmation from courier delivery communication. If the customer has already received the parcel, stop obsolete order-confirmation prompts. If they canceled, follow the actual cancellation process rather than continuing persuasion by default.

## Build a task-based ledger

| Order state | Useful contact purpose |
| Missing detail | Resolve the specific information gap |
| Awaiting confirmation | Verify the actual purchase expectations |
| Confirmed | Explain the supported next step if needed |
| Delivery issue | Resolve the recorded operational problem |
| Delivered or canceled | Stop messages for the old task |

Use the appropriate channel permissions and recorded customer preferences. Do not prescribe one universal number of attempts for every situation.

## Worked example: three agents ask the same question

Illustrative example: an order enters a call queue and two message sequences. The customer confirms by phone, but the sequences still ask for confirmation. A shared state and send-time check prevent these redundant requests.

A frequency cap alone may postpone the outdated message until tomorrow. The more direct repair is to exit the completed task and preserve the agreement in the order record.

## Measure resolution and downstream outcomes

Review clarification completed, confirmation accuracy, customer complaints and mature deliveries. More answered calls do not necessarily mean better customer fit. Investigate why contact was needed and whether the original page could answer the question earlier.

Audit a few orders across every team and channel. Identify duplicated tasks and assign the next action to one owner. The [frequency-cap guide](lifecycle-frequency-caps.html) helps coordinate sequences without hiding necessary operational communication.
